How to Sign Up

Follow these simple steps to register and start managing your students seamlessly on HeyKudu.

1

Go to heykudu.com

Visit our website on any device to sign up.

2

Complete Profile

Fill in your details and click the Complete Profile button.

3

Important for Consultants

Make sure you toggle ON the Educator Supervisor Control and select the correct role.

4

Scan to Verify

To sign off on a student's work, simply ask them to show you the QR code for their submission and scan it with your phone. This counts as your official signature.

Need detailed rotation access?

Email no-reply@heykudu.com to request full access for the entire rotation.
