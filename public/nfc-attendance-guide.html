Scan NFC Attendance Tags

Follow these simple instructions to log your attendance seamlessly using HeyKudu's NFC tags.

For iPhone (iOS)

  • • Wake your phone – the screen must be on (lock screen or unlocked).
  • • Tap the TOP edge of your iPhone (near the camera) directly against the tag.
  • • Tap the pop-up notification "Open in Safari" to record your attendance.

For Android

  • • Turn NFC ON – swipe down from the top of your screen to check Quick Settings.
  • • Unlock your phone.
  • • Tap the CENTER of the back of your phone against the tag.
  • • Tap the notification to open in Chrome.

Check-In & Check-Out

  • Arriving at session Tap once ➔ Check-In
  • Leaving session Tap again when finished ➔ Check-Out

Accidental double-taps within 5 minutes will NOT create duplicates.

Quick Troubleshooting

  • Enable Android NFC Search "NFC" in your phone Settings to turn it on if it's missing from Quick Settings.
  • Thick or magnetic cases Remove thick wallet cases or metal phone grips if your phone isn't detecting the tag.
  • Still not working? If scanning fails, you can manually check in via heykudu.com on your browser.
