# MailerSend Sequence Architecture Skill

## Purpose
Generates high-converting, low-friction email sequences that respect clinician time and prompt executive conversations.

## 3-Touch Sequence Structure

### Touch 1: The Operational Reality Check (Day 0)
- **Subject Line Formula**: `[Specific Department / Pain Point] at [University Name]` (e.g., "Paediatrics clinical logbooks at UCT").
- **Body**: 120 words maximum. Call out the reality of paper logbook administration during rotation weeks. Reference how Wits digitized GEMP 2.
- **CTA**: "Open to seeing a 2-minute screen recording of how Wits handles this?"

### Touch 2: The Accreditation & Safety Angle (Day 3)
- **Subject Line Formula**: `HPCSA audit readiness & ward presence`
- **Body**: Focus on risk mitigation: ghost attendance, missing signatures, and lost portfolios. Explain how 1-tap NFC check-in provides tamper-proof verification.
- **CTA**: "Would you like me to share the 1-page departmental rollout framework?"

### Touch 3: The 15-Minute Convenor Walkthrough (Day 7)
- **Subject Line Formula**: `quick thought for [First Name]`
- **Body**: Direct and low pressure. Note that clinical departments can run a zero-friction 1-block pilot without changing their existing curriculum.
- **CTA**: "If this is relevant for your next semester rotation, let's grab 15 minutes."
