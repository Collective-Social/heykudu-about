# Institutional Dossier Research Skill

## Purpose
Gathers targeted intelligence on medical faculty deaneries, rotation convenors, and affiliated teaching hospitals.

## Research Checklist
1. **Target University & Faculty Profile**:
   - Institutional Name & Key Campuses (e.g., UCT Faculty of Health Sciences, Observatory).
   - Core Affiliated Teaching Hospitals (e.g., Groote Schuur, Red Cross Children's, Victoria Hospital).
   - Student cohort size per year (e.g., ~250 MBChB clinical students per year).
2. **Current Administrative Friction**:
   - Are they still using paper workbooks or ad-hoc Google Forms?
   - What is the clinical rotation structure (block duration, clinical disciplines)?
3. **Executive Stakeholders**:
   - Dean / Deputy Dean of Teaching and Learning.
   - Head of Department (HOD) for Obstetrics & Gynaecology, Paediatrics, Internal Medicine, Surgery.
   - Clinical Rotation Coordinators.
4. **Tailored Strategic Angle**:
   - Direct connection to their specific hospital rotation setup (e.g., "Managing rotation shifts across Groote Schuur and Red Cross without paper registers").
