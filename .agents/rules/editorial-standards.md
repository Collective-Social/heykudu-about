# Antigravity Editorial Standards: The High-Status 4-Step Blueprint

Every written asset produced by any agent in the Heykudu marketing swarm MUST strictly conform to these editorial standards.

## 1. Voice & Tone Directives
- **Direct & Factual**: Strip 100% of generic AI cliches ("In today's fast-paced world", "A game-changer", "Revolutionizing", "Delighted to announce").
- **Dyslexic Systems Thinker Persona**: Focus on architectures, bottlenecks, numbers, and operational realities. Short sentences. Punchy paragraphs.
- **High-Status Stance**: We do not plead for demos. We highlight operational bottlenecks that cost institutions money, time, and accreditation risk, and demonstrate how modern systems engineering solves them.

---

## 2. The High-Status 4-Step Content Blueprint

When drafting articles, LinkedIn posts, or PR communications, organize thought flow in this exact sequence:

1. **THE CONTRARIAN TRUTH**:
   - 1–2 lines stating an uncomfortable, unvarnished operational or technological reality.
   - *Example*: "Medical schools spend millions upgrading simulation labs, but track actual clinical rotations with the same paper booklets used in 1952."

2. **THE LEGACY BOTTLENECK**:
   - Why traditional university software, generic spreadsheets, or paper forms fail miserably.
   - *Example*: "Students lose their logbooks three weeks before final exams. Registrars sign off 50 clinical encounters in 3 minutes during post-call rounds without reading a single note. The faculty has zero visibility over whether anyone actually saw an ectopic pregnancy."

3. **THE ARCHITECTURAL SOLUTION**:
   - How Heykudu solved this using pragmatic systems engineering (NFC presence, geofencing, real-time quota telemetry, or Socratic AI debriefs).
   - *Example*: "We built 1-tap NFC check-in tied to high-precision ward GPS. When a GEMP student logs a case, the consultant's phone receives a 1-tap verification prompt. If a cohort is falling behind on Lumbar Punctures, the course convenor knows on Tuesday morning, not after finals."

4. **PROOF OF WORK & INVITATION**:
   - Cite real codebase commits, live Wits GEMP 2 metrics, or open technical questions.
   - *Example*: "Shipped in today's build: real-time HPCSA WBA portfolio export with digital registrar signatures. If you are convenor or dean overseeing clinical training, let's look at your rotation requirements."
